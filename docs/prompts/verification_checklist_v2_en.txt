General decision verification checklist:
1. Identify the user's objective, mandatory conditions, and preferences for ranking eligible options. Distinguish facts, conditions, authorization, and suggestions; do not add assumptions absent from the task.
2. Check candidates against all applicable conditions. Do not mistake partial satisfaction for overall satisfaction or omit circumstances that can affect the purchase.
3. Verify amounts, units, strict versus inclusive thresholds, and the order of fees and compensation. Valid simplification, grouping, and bounds may reduce repeated calculations, but unverified approximations must not replace exact boundary judgments.
4. Compare eligible candidates and verify that the selected option agrees with its reported utility. Refuse only when there is no positive-value option meeting the task requirements; failure to find an option is not itself evidence of infeasibility.
5. Before submitting, check whether the selected option meets every relevant requirement, whether a better eligible option was missed, and whether refusal is justified. Return only the required answer format and a short reason.
Make the decision directly and return the result and a short reason in the specified format.
