\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Uncertainty and consequential decisions.}
KnowNo, IntentSim, and AmbiK examine uncertainty and clarification in planning \citep{knowno,intentsim,ambik}. VoI weighs the utility of information against user effort, while SAGE structures uncertainty over tool parameters \citep{voi,sage}. Related studies address abstention, ambiguous questions, and formal uncertainty \citep{sconu,solveuncertainty,wrongquestion,gofu}. RAC connects prediction sets to risk-averse decisions; risk-control and action-conditional extensions add statistical guarantees under their assumptions \citep{rac,crc,nonxcrc,actionrac,clm}. \citet{uncertaingeneration} apply Bayesian and risk-averse decisions to tutoring and reviewing. We use the inherited decision principle for executable purchases with explicit financial permissions, a common decision across states, and an outside option. The operating condition excludes further clarification; it does not imply that prior interaction methods cannot act under uncertainty.

\paragraph{Executable reasoning and formalization.}
PAL, Logic-LM, LINC, and Logic.py delegate computation to interpreters, provers, or solvers \citep{pal,logiclm,linc,logicpy}. Formalization studies investigate the fidelity of language-to-logic translation and its sensitivity to natural descriptions \citep{folcode,fover,planninglimit,verify}. We inherit the separation of compilation and execution, with state-dependent purchasing conditions as the generated object. Separating stable requirements from catalogue data also limits the need to repeatedly process large contexts \citep{lostmiddle}. Our evaluation measures the entire decision architecture; supplementary execution controls distinguish shared computational benefits from representation-dependent failures.

\paragraph{Constrained agents and evaluation.}
GuardAgent generates executable safeguards, DeAction corrects proposed actions, AgentSpec enforces structured runtime constraints, and VeriGuard verifies policies and actions \citep{guardagent,deaction,agentspec,veriguard}. AutoCO studies executable constraint-relaxation strategies; financial metric graphs constrain derivations \citep{autoco,financialgraph}. Our focus is the completion/refusal boundary of a purchase under retained states, including separate admissibility and value conditions. PlanBench, CoPE, FollowBench, ComplexBench, and AgentIF test planning or composed instructions \citep{planbench,cope,followbench,complexbench,agentif}; ToolEmu, AgentDojo, and AgentHarm assess consequential or adversarial agent behavior \citep{toolemu,agentdojo,agentharm}. We contribute controlled contractual boundaries and semantic diagnostics, drawing on behavioral testing and contrastive evaluation \citep{checklist,hans,contrastsets}. Authorization-domain auditing supplements decision evaluation within this task.
